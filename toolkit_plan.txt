PERSONAL MINI-TOOLKIT — PROJECT PLAN

Chosen Tools (3):
1. Quick Task List: Lets the user add tasks, view their current list, and remove completed tasks during the session.
2. Daily Expense & Budget Check: Adds up expense amounts entered by the user and checks how they compare against a daily spending target.
3. Study Session Timer: Counts down whole minutes or seconds in the terminal until the study block finishes.

Exact Menu Text:
========================================
        PERSONAL MINI-TOOLKIT
========================================
1. Quick Task List
2. Daily Expense & Budget Check
3. Study Session Timer
4. Quit
----------------------------------------
Choose an option (1-4):

Concepts Used per Tool:
- Quick Task List: while/for loops, mutable list (append, pop/remove, indexing), conditionals (if/elif/else), f-strings.
- Daily Expense & Budget Check: for/while loop (collecting amounts and summing), conditional (if/elif/else comparing total to budget), f-strings.
- Study Session Timer: while loop with countdown and time.sleep(1), conditional (input validation and completion message), f-strings.
